# ARGUS Privacy and Safety Stance

ARGUS is designed to reconstruct events, not to determine guilt.

## Core Rules

1. **No Facial Recognition**: We do not identify individuals by name or cross-reference with identity databases.
2. **No Biometric Identity**: Re-ID is based on appearance (clothing, color) over short timeframes, not persistent biometrics.
3. **No Criminal Labeling**: The system must never label a person as a "criminal", "thief", or "suspect".
4. **Preferred Terminology**: Use "subject", "person", "candidate trajectory", or "relevant individual".
5. **Human Review**: ARGUS provides evidence for human review. It is not an autonomous judge.
6. **Uncertainty is First-Class**: The system must display the "UNKNOWN" gaps and "INFERRED" deductions. Do not hide the system's uncertainty.
